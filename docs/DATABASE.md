# Kaam Setu — Central Database Design & Schema

Kaam Setu uses **Prisma ORM** with a single authoritative relational database shared between both Customer and Worker applications.

---

## 1. Relational Entity-Relationship Model

### Core Entities:

1. **`User`**
   - Central identity table for both Customers and Workers.
   - Fields: `id`, `name`, `email` (unique), `phone`, `passwordHash`, `role` (`CUSTOMER` | `WORKER` | `ADMIN`), `avatarUrl`, `status`.

2. **`CustomerProfile`**
   - 1:1 relation with `User` where `role == CUSTOMER`.
   - Fields: `id`, `userId`, `preferredAddress`.

3. **`WorkerProfile`**
   - 1:1 relation with `User` where `role == WORKER`.
   - Fields: `id`, `userId`, `bio`, `skills` (JSON), `serviceRadiusKm`, `hourlyRate`, `baseRate`, `isAvailable` (boolean toggle), `ratingAvg`, `ratingCount`, `completedJobsCount`.

4. **`WorkerVerification`**
   - 1:many relation with `WorkerProfile`.
   - Fields: `id`, `workerId`, `type` (`PHONE` | `ID_DOCUMENT` | `SKILL_CERT` | `BACKGROUND_CHECK`), `status` (`VERIFIED` | `PENDING` | `REJECTED`), `notes`, `reviewedAt`.

5. **`ServiceCategory`**
   - Standardized trade categories.
   - Fields: `id`, `name`, `slug` (unique), `description`, `icon`, `active`, `basePriceEstimate`.

6. **`WorkerService`**
   - Many-to-many junction between `WorkerProfile` and `ServiceCategory`.
   - Fields: `id`, `workerId`, `serviceCategoryId`, `experienceYears`, `customRate`.
   - Unique constraint: `@@unique([workerId, serviceCategoryId])`.

7. **`ServiceLocation`**
   - User address and geo-coordinates for radius calculations.
   - Fields: `id`, `userId`, `label`, `address`, `city`, `postalCode`, `latitude`, `longitude`, `isDefault`.

8. **`JobRequest`**
   - Service requests created by customers.
   - Fields: `id`, `customerId`, `serviceCategoryId`, `title`, `description`, `addressLabel`, `city`, `latitude`, `longitude`, `urgency` (`ASAP` | `SCHEDULED`), `budgetMin`, `budgetMax`, `isOpenToQuotes`, `status` (`OPEN` | `MATCHING` | `OFFERS_RECEIVED` | `BOOKED` | `COMPLETED` | `CANCELLED`).

9. **`JobOffer`**
   - Durable job offers generated by the Fair Matching Engine for eligible workers.
   - Fields: `id`, `jobRequestId`, `workerId`, `status` (`PENDING` | `VIEWED` | `QUOTED` | `DECLINED` | `EXPIRED`), `matchScore`, `matchReasons` (JSON array), `sentAt`, `expiresAt`, `respondedAt`.
   - Unique constraint: `@@unique([jobRequestId, workerId])` prevents duplicate dispatches.

10. **`Quote`**
    - Formal price quote submitted by a worker for a job request.
    - Fields: `id`, `jobRequestId`, `workerId`, `proposedPrice`, `estimatedArrivalMinutes`, `scopeDescription`, `exclusions`, `workerNote`, `status` (`PENDING` | `COUNTEROFFERED` | `ACCEPTED` | `REJECTED`).

11. **`QuoteNegotiation`**
    - Itemized negotiation history between customer and worker.
    - Fields: `id`, `quoteId`, `senderId`, `senderRole`, `proposedPrice`, `message`, `createdAt`.

12. **`Booking`**
    - Authoritative binding contract between customer and worker upon quote acceptance.
    - Fields: `id`, `jobRequestId` (unique), `customerId`, `workerId`, `acceptedQuoteId` (unique), `agreedPrice`, `agreedScope`, `startOtp` (4-digit security code), `status` (`CONFIRMED` | `ON_THE_WAY` | `WORK_STARTED` | `COMPLETED` | `CANCELLED`), `startedAt`, `completedAt`, `cancellationReason`.

13. **`Notification`**
    - Durable in-app notification center.
    - Fields: `id`, `recipientUserId`, `type`, `title`, `message`, `relatedEntityType`, `relatedEntityId`, `readAt`.

14. **`Review`**
    - Customer feedback and star rating (1-5) for completed bookings.
    - Fields: `id`, `bookingId` (unique), `reviewerId`, `revieweeId`, `rating`, `comment`.

15. **`SupportTicket`**
    - Safety and dispute tickets for customers and workers.
    - Fields: `id`, `userId`, `bookingId`, `category`, `subject`, `description`, `status`.

## 2. Production PostgreSQL Deployment

To deploy to a production PostgreSQL database (Supabase, Neon, AWS RDS, Render, etc.):

1. In `server/.env`, set:
   ```env
   DATABASE_URL="postgresql://user:password@host:5432/hyperlocal?schema=public"
   ```
2. Replace `server/prisma/schema.prisma` with `server/prisma/schema.postgresql.prisma`.
3. Run:
   ```bash
   npx prisma migrate dev --name init
   npx tsx prisma/seed.ts
   ```

### Extended Safety, Credibility & Verification Entities:

16. **`CustomerEmergencyContact`**
    - Trusted emergency contacts (max 3 per customer).
    - Fields: `id`, `customerId`, `name`, `relationship`, `phone`, `email`, `priority` (1-3), `isVerified`, `verifiedAt`, `createdAt`, `updatedAt`.

17. **`CustomerCareSetting`**
    - Platform support & emergency helpline configurations.
    - Fields: `id`, `settingKey` (unique), `settingValue`, `description`, `updatedAt`.

18. **`SafetyEvent`**
    - Persistent audit trail of SOS triggers and emergency alerts.
    - Fields: `id`, `customerId`, `bookingId`, `eventType` (`SOS_PANIC` | `SAFETY_CHECKIN_TIMEOUT` | `DISPUTE_ESCALATION`), `status` (`ACTIVE` | `RESOLVED` | `CANCELLED`), `locationLat`, `locationLng`, `notes`, `triggeredAt`, `resolvedAt`, `resolvedBy`.

19. **`SafetyCheckIn`**
    - Scheduled proactive check-in milestones for active bookings.
    - Fields: `id`, `bookingId`, `milestoneType` (`WORKER_DEPARTED` | `WORKER_ARRIVED` | `WORK_STARTED` | `OVERDUE_COMPLETION`), `expectedAt`, `status` (`SCHEDULED` | `REMINDER_SENT` | `WARNING_SENT` | `ESCALATED` | `ACKNOWLEDGED`), `escalationStage` (0-3), `acknowledgedAt`, `createdAt`.

20. **`EmergencyCallLog`**
    - Automated telephony call dispatches to emergency contacts.
    - Fields: `id`, `safetyEventId`, `recipientPhone`, `recipientName`, `status` (`INITIATED` | `RINGING` | `IN_PROGRESS` | `COMPLETED` | `FAILED`), `callSid`, `provider` (`MOCK` | `TWILIO` | `EXOTEL`), `recordingUrl`, `initiatedAt`, `completedAt`, `errorDetails`.

21. **`WorkerIdentityDocument`**
    - Government ID verification (Aadhaar, Voter ID, PAN, DL, Passport).
    - Fields: `id`, `workerId`, `documentType` (`AADHAAR` | `VOTER_ID` | `PAN` | `DRIVING_LICENSE` | `PASSPORT`), `documentIdentifierMasked`, `identifierHash` (SHA-256 for duplicate check), `documentFrontUrl`, `documentBackUrl`, `status` (`PENDING` | `VERIFIED` | `REJECTED`), `reviewerNotes`, `reviewedBy`, `verifiedAt`.

22. **`WorkerSkillEvidence`**
    - Formal vocational/trade certificates (ITI, NSDC, Skill India, Polytechnic).
    - Fields: `id`, `workerId`, `tradeCategory`, `certificateName`, `issuingAuthority`, `certificateNumber`, `issueYear`, `documentUrl`, `qrPayload`, `verificationStatus` (`PENDING` | `VERIFIED_BY_ISSUER` | `REJECTED`), `verifiedAt`.

23. **`WorkerTermsAcceptance`**
    - Timestamped legal acceptance of the Worker Rights & Dignity Charter.
    - Fields: `id`, `workerId`, `termsVersion`, `acceptedAt`, `ipAddress`, `userAgent`, `legalNoticeCited` ("Code on Social Security 2020").

24. **`WorkerRPLApplication`**
    - Recognition of Prior Learning portfolio for experienced informal workers.
    - Fields: `id`, `workerId`, `tradeCategory`, `experienceYears`, `priorLearningSummary`, `referencesJson`, `assessmentStatus` (`SUBMITTED` | `UNDER_REVIEW` | `APPROVED` | `REJECTED`), `reviewerNotes`, `submittedAt`, `reviewedAt`.

25. **`WorkerBadge`**
    - Genuine, server-computed credibility and milestone badges.
    - Fields: `id`, `workerId`, `badgeKey` (`FIRST_JOB` | `JOBS_10` | `JOBS_25` | `EXPERIENCED_PRO` | `CERT_*` | `RPL_RECOGNIZED`), `title`, `description`, `icon`, `category` (`EXPERIENCE` | `SKILL_CERTIFICATE` | `RPL` | `SAFETY`), `awardedAt`.

26. **`VisitingCharge`**
    - Worker-defined diagnostic and callout pricing.
    - Fields: `id`, `workerId` (unique), `amount` (₹), `isAdjustableAgainstFinalBill` (boolean), `termsDescription`, `updatedAt`.

27. **`BookingChargeAcknowledgement`**
    - Audit record of customer's explicit pre-booking visiting fee acknowledgement.
    - Fields: `id`, `bookingId` (unique), `customerId`, `visitingCharge`, `acknowledgedAt`, `ipAddress`.

28. **`CertificateVerificationRecord`**
    - Cryptographic QR and DigiLocker validation logs.
    - Fields: `id`, `certificateNumber`, `issuingAuthority`, `verificationMethod` (`QR_CODE` | `DIGILOCKER` | `MANUAL`), `isValid`, `verificationDataJson`, `verifiedAt`.

29. **`DuplicateAccountFlag`**
    - Risk scoring and audit records for shared identity/device/phone credentials.
    - Fields: `id`, `userId`, `matchedUserId`, `flagReason`, `riskScore` (`LOW` | `MEDIUM` | `HIGH`), `status` (`PENDING_REVIEW` | `RESOLVED_ALLOWED` | `BLOCKED`), `createdAt`, `resolvedAt`.
