# HyperLocal — Central Database Design & Schema

HyperLocal uses **Prisma ORM** with a single authoritative relational database shared between both Customer and Worker applications.

---

## 1. Relational Entity-Relationship Model

### Core Entities:

1. **`User`**
   - Central identity table for both Customers and Workers.
   - Fields: `id`, `name`, `email` (unique), `phone`, `passwordHash`, `role` (`CUSTOMER` | `WORKER` | `ADMIN`), `avatarUrl`, `status`.

2. **`CustomerProfile`**
   - 1:1 relation with `User` where `role == CUSTOMER`.
   - Fields: `id`, `userId`, `preferredAddress`.

3. **`WorkerProfile`**
   - 1:1 relation with `User` where `role == WORKER`.
   - Fields: `id`, `userId`, `bio`, `skills` (JSON), `serviceRadiusKm`, `hourlyRate`, `baseRate`, `isAvailable` (boolean toggle), `ratingAvg`, `ratingCount`, `completedJobsCount`.

4. **`WorkerVerification`**
   - 1:many relation with `WorkerProfile`.
   - Fields: `id`, `workerId`, `type` (`PHONE` | `ID_DOCUMENT` | `SKILL_CERT` | `BACKGROUND_CHECK`), `status` (`VERIFIED` | `PENDING` | `REJECTED`), `notes`, `reviewedAt`.

5. **`ServiceCategory`**
   - Standardized trade categories.
   - Fields: `id`, `name`, `slug` (unique), `description`, `icon`, `active`, `basePriceEstimate`.

6. **`WorkerService`**
   - Many-to-many junction between `WorkerProfile` and `ServiceCategory`.
   - Fields: `id`, `workerId`, `serviceCategoryId`, `experienceYears`, `customRate`.
   - Unique constraint: `@@unique([workerId, serviceCategoryId])`.

7. **`ServiceLocation`**
   - User address and geo-coordinates for radius calculations.
   - Fields: `id`, `userId`, `label`, `address`, `city`, `postalCode`, `latitude`, `longitude`, `isDefault`.

8. **`JobRequest`**
   - Service requests created by customers.
   - Fields: `id`, `customerId`, `serviceCategoryId`, `title`, `description`, `addressLabel`, `city`, `latitude`, `longitude`, `urgency` (`ASAP` | `SCHEDULED`), `budgetMin`, `budgetMax`, `isOpenToQuotes`, `status` (`OPEN` | `MATCHING` | `OFFERS_RECEIVED` | `BOOKED` | `COMPLETED` | `CANCELLED`).

9. **`JobOffer`**
   - Durable job offers generated by the Fair Matching Engine for eligible workers.
   - Fields: `id`, `jobRequestId`, `workerId`, `status` (`PENDING` | `VIEWED` | `QUOTED` | `DECLINED` | `EXPIRED`), `matchScore`, `matchReasons` (JSON array), `sentAt`, `expiresAt`, `respondedAt`.
   - Unique constraint: `@@unique([jobRequestId, workerId])` prevents duplicate dispatches.

10. **`Quote`**
    - Formal price quote submitted by a worker for a job request.
    - Fields: `id`, `jobRequestId`, `workerId`, `proposedPrice`, `estimatedArrivalMinutes`, `scopeDescription`, `exclusions`, `workerNote`, `status` (`PENDING` | `COUNTEROFFERED` | `ACCEPTED` | `REJECTED`).

11. **`QuoteNegotiation`**
    - Itemized negotiation history between customer and worker.
    - Fields: `id`, `quoteId`, `senderId`, `senderRole`, `proposedPrice`, `message`, `createdAt`.

12. **`Booking`**
    - Authoritative binding contract between customer and worker upon quote acceptance.
    - Fields: `id`, `jobRequestId` (unique), `customerId`, `workerId`, `acceptedQuoteId` (unique), `agreedPrice`, `agreedScope`, `startOtp` (4-digit security code), `status` (`CONFIRMED` | `ON_THE_WAY` | `WORK_STARTED` | `COMPLETED` | `CANCELLED`), `startedAt`, `completedAt`, `cancellationReason`.

13. **`Notification`**
    - Durable in-app notification center.
    - Fields: `id`, `recipientUserId`, `type`, `title`, `message`, `relatedEntityType`, `relatedEntityId`, `readAt`.

14. **`Review`**
    - Customer feedback and star rating (1-5) for completed bookings.
    - Fields: `id`, `bookingId` (unique), `reviewerId`, `revieweeId`, `rating`, `comment`.

15. **`SupportTicket`**
    - Safety and dispute tickets for customers and workers.
    - Fields: `id`, `userId`, `bookingId`, `category`, `subject`, `description`, `status`.

---

## 2. Production PostgreSQL Deployment

To deploy to a production PostgreSQL database (Supabase, Neon, AWS RDS, Render, etc.):

1. In `server/.env`, set:
   ```env
   DATABASE_URL="postgresql://user:password@host:5432/hyperlocal?schema=public"
   ```
2. Replace `server/prisma/schema.prisma` with `server/prisma/schema.postgresql.prisma`.
3. Run:
   ```bash
   npx prisma migrate dev --name init
   npx tsx prisma/seed.ts
   ```
